This website

This is an informational and download website, not an online vault. It has no user accounts, upload forms, advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or embedded third-party fonts. It does not access your vault, receive vault passwords, or operate a chat relay.

On a published GitHub Pages site, GitHub processes requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security. APK and source downloads are served by GitHub Releases and its infrastructure. Their processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement. Your browser, network provider, and operating system may keep their own history or logs. We do not control those records.

Feature searches and category filters run entirely in your browser. Search text is not sent to a server, added to the URL, or saved in browser storage. The optional Copy checksum button writes only the displayed public checksum to your clipboard when you press it. All essential information and links remain available without JavaScript. External links take you to services with their own terms; this site sends no referrer header.

The app: local first, network optional

Files and passwords

Imported files are stored as encrypted data in app-private internal storage. Sensitive filenames and folder metadata are encrypted. Per-file authenticated encryption uses AES-256-GCM; password-derived key protection and Android Keystore protect the vault keys. Passwords are not stored in plaintext or reversible form.

Viewing decrypts the required chunks into memory or an explicitly authorized stream. Decoded previews can exist temporarily in RAM. Exporting or opening with another app can deliver plaintext outside the vault. Files are not all decrypted into an unprotected folder just because you unlock.

Connections you choose

Opt-in private chats use the embedded SimpleX Chat core and third-party message/file relays. Messages are end-to-end encrypted; vault attachments also use an encrypted envelope. Relays and network peers can observe connection metadata such as addresses, timing, and transfer sizes. Availability and retention depend on the relay and its conditions. Chat sessions stop when the vault locks; no locked-screen chat notifications or cloud chat recovery are provided.

Nearby sharing uses an explicitly paired local connection. A chat's Nearby preferred option uses relay signaling and may fall back to relay delivery; the separate offline pairing flow does not require the internet. Group transfers use relays only. Explicit public-link imports contact the relevant public service, which has its own policies. There are no analytics, ads, or automatic cloud library synchronization.

Permissions and data leaving the vault

Android's file picker grants access to files or folders you select. Network access supports the optional features above; local-network access may require an Android permission. Notifications and exact-alarm access support clock alarms and timers, subject to device settings. There is no broad photo-library access or location requirement for Nearby.

Source-file deletion is offered explicitly after a verified import, never performed automatically. Providers may refuse deletion or retain their own copies. You control explicit exports and password-encrypted portable library backups. Android automatic backup/device transfer is disabled; keep your own tested backup.

The camera permission is optional and requested only for the in-app Nearby QR scanner. Camera preview frames are not saved. Pairing codes can be pasted instead.

Some video editing and conversion operations require seekable temporary plaintext files in app-private cache storage. They are cleaned up on success/failure and leftovers are cleared on the next process start. A crash, forced stop, or flash wear-leveling can leave recoverable traces; this cleanup is not guaranteed physical secure erasure. Image editing uses in-memory working buffers and saves new encrypted copies.

Security has boundaries

Safe Mode locks on leaving the app, revokes owned content grants, and removes the task from Recents. Temporary Normal Mode has a weaker privacy posture: keys remain in process memory while backgrounded until screen-off restores Safe Mode. Android controls process lifetime and memory reclamation; the app cannot guarantee immediate process termination or complete memory erasure.

Screenshot protection uses Android's supported secure-window mechanisms. It is not a defense against every privileged capture method, an external camera, malware, a rooted/compromised OS, or a malicious accessibility service. An unlocked app necessarily handles decrypted data in memory.

Deletion is not guaranteed physical erasure from flash storage. Recycled files remain until purged. Removing a group member, cancelling a transfer, or deleting a local item cannot retract copies that recipients, providers, or other apps already hold. Public documentation and source also mean the existence of vault functionality is discoverable.

No password recovery: losing your password or installation keys may permanently lose access. A separately created backup requires its own password and does not include chat identity/history. Uninstalling or clearing app data destroys the current installation's keys.

These are design descriptions, not an independent security certification, a guarantee against forensic examination, or a promise of safety in every threat model.
